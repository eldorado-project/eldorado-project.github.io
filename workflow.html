Open-Pit Gold Mine Investment Milestones

Risk Investment Bonus Distribution (of Distributed Production)

Total Risk Investment Bonus = 16% of Distributed Production

1. Licensing & Permitting

Capital Allocation: 2% – 5%
Securing mining licenses, land rights, environmental approvals, and regulatory compliance.
Investor Protection: Legal due diligence, license verification, and restricted capital release until permits are fully validated.
Risk Level: Very High
Risk Investment Bonus: 7%

2. Geological Testing & Resource Evaluation

Capital Allocation: 8% – 12%
Drilling, sampling, geological modeling, and resource estimation under international standards.
Investor Protection: Independent geological audits, JORC / NI 43-101 reporting, and milestone-based funding tranches.
Risk Level: High
Risk Investment Bonus: 5%

3. Proof of Reserves & Feasibility

Capital Allocation: 5% – 8%
Conversion of resources into proven and probable reserves through feasibility studies and economic modeling.
Investor Protection: Third-party feasibility validation, conservative assumptions, and investment approval gates.
Risk Level: Medium
Risk Investment Bonus: 3%

4. Mining Infrastructure Development

Capital Allocation: 40% – 50%
Core capital deployment phase: site prep, haul roads, fleet acquisition, power & water supply, workshops, and tailings systems.
  • Mine Site Preparation
  • Mining Fleet & Equipment
  • Power Infrastructure
  • Water Management Systems
  • Support Facilities
  • Environmental & Safety Systems
Investor Protection: Escrow-controlled payments, EPC contracts, insurance coverage, independent inspections.
Risk Level: Medium-Low
Risk Investment Bonus: 1%

5. Processing Plant & Refinery Integration

Capital Allocation: 20% – 25%
Installation of crushing, milling, recovery systems, and refinery or dore production facilities.
Investor Protection: Independent metallurgical testing, assay reconciliation, and transparent refining contracts.
Risk Level: Low
Risk Investment Bonus: 0%

6. Gold Production & Operational Scaling

Capital Allocation: 5% – 10%
Commercial production, cost optimization, and scaling of gold output.
Investor Protection: Audited production reports, segregated revenue accounts, and continuous compliance monitoring.
Risk Level: Minimal
Risk Investment Bonus: 0%
