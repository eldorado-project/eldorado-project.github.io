Why Algorithmic Trading?

Automated trading has become a ky option for traders seeking more consistent and disciplined results in financial markets, especially given rapid market fluctuations and the challenges of relying solely on manual trading. Manual trading is often influenced by emotions, hesitation, and fear of loss, leading to impulsive decisions and repeated losses. In contrast, automated trading relies on predefined systems and strategies that are executed accurately without being affected by emotions or psychological pressure.

  • 24/7 Trading

Manual Trading vs. Automated Trading

A regular trader operates within the limits of human capacity, typically able to monitor only 2–3 indicators simultaneously, and requires several seconds to minutes to analyze and make decisions for each trading opportunity. This delay can result in missed optimal entry and exit points. Additionally, manual trading cannot be sustained beyond 8–10 hours per day and is affected by fatigue and distraction, increasing the likelihood of poor decisions. According to market statistics, individual traders spend over 60% of their time analyzing data rather than making precise decisions.

In contrast, an automated trader can process more than 20 indicators and variables simultaneously, execute orders within fractions of a second, and operate 24/7 without any performance decline. Using multiple indicators allows for cross-verification of signals (Signal Confirmation), reducing false signals by up to 70% compared to manual trading, according to backtesting results of quantitative trading systems.

The key difference is that automated trading relies on system speed and programming accuracy rather than human capabilities, giving it a clear advantage in fast-moving and highly competitive markets.
