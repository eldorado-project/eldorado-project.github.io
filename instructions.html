Article (1): Create Account

The investor creates a new account on the platform by entering the following information:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Country
  • Valid official document (ID or Passport)
  • Password

A confirmation email is sent to the registered email. Email confirmation is required to complete registration.

Article (2): Activate Account

  • After confirming the email, the investor can log into the account.
  • A personal wallet number and a subscription wallet number are created.
  • The account remains inactive until the subscription amount is sent.
  • The account is activated within 48 to 72 hours from receiving the subscription.

Article (3): Choose Project and Invest

  • The investor selects an investment project from the available projects.
  • The investment amount is deposited into the selected project wallet.
  • The investment amount is refundable according to the approved project terms.

Article (4): Investment Certificate and Digital Document

  • After completing the investment, the investor receives an official investment certificate.
  • A digital investment document is issued, linked to the investor and the project.
  • The certificate and digital document include a unique investment code.
  • The certificate and digital document are sent to the investor’s country according to the approved procedure.
  • They serve as official proof of the investor's rights.
